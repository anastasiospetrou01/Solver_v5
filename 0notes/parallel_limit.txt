| Finding                                    | Evidence and consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nonlinear stopping is incomplete           | The stopping rule checks `mass_residual`, plus `dT_inf` for energy cases. It records \(u,v,p\) changes but does not require them—or momentum equation residuals—to converge. Low divergence alone does not establish a steady momentum solution.                                                                                                                |
| Energy assembly omits \(c_p\)              | The active energy kernel combines \(\dot m\) with thermal conductance \(kA/d\), without multiplying convection by \(c_p\). Materials provide physical conductivity and heat capacity, while energy reporting uses \(\dot m c_pT\). Assembly and reporting therefore represent different energy balances. Open-boundary advective energy terms also need review. |
| Buoyancy correction has inconsistent signs | The reconstructed vertical flux adds the buoyancy interpolation correction. Its divergence should enter the continuity RHS with the opposite sign. The active assembly instead adds the same signed contribution.                                                                                                                                               |
