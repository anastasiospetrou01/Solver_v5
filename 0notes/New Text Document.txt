18. Practical interpretation of your current solver

The current method is best described as:

Monolithic, fully coupled [u,v,p]
Picard/Oseen nonlinear linearization
Defect-correction update
Rhie–Chow collocated continuity
FGMRES + PCD block preconditioning

It is more strongly coupled than SIMPLE-like segregated methods because one linear solve contains all u,v,p variables.

But it is less strongly nonlinear-coupled than full Newton because some nonlinear dependencies are frozen and updated only between outer iterations.
